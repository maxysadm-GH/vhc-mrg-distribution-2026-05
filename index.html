06 · Read-Out

What the data is telling us.

01

Distribution depth is genuinely expanding.

Sixty-nine percent of locations show a positive SKU-breadth slope across the period — not from one outlier, but as a broad trend. Forty-eight new ship-tos came online in the most recent three months, while only eight from the opening window have gone dormant. Both axes (more locations, more SKUs per location) are moving the right direction.

02

March 2026 was a step-change, not a drift.

Thirty-two ship-tos activated in a single month — more than any prior month. This is a discrete onboarding wave from MRG. We should confirm with them which roll-out cycle this corresponds to and whether the same cohort is on track to add SKU breadth from their initial pull, or whether it stays at base assortment.

03

The assortment is the ceiling.

Seven active SKUs explains the entire chart. Five 3oz bars carry 84% of volume; the 6PC caramel and 4PC truffle do the rest. Pink Himalayan Salt Caramel leads at 22%. The expansion story is real, but every location maxes out at seven SKUs by definition. If the goal is meaningful depth growth beyond the current trajectory, the lever is adding SKUs to the airport assortment — not adding doors.

04

Concentration is healthy.

HHI of 230 is well below the 1,500 threshold for "low concentration." The top single location accounts for 6.1% of volume; top twenty stores for 58%. No single airport closure or remodel can collapse the account.

05

The numbers reconcile across systems.

Fishbowl issued 232 orders to MRG/WH Smith ZIPs since July; ShipStation/Supabase confirms 229 shipments. The 3-order gap is the in-flight pipeline (FB issues first, SS ships later). Carrier mix is 79% FedEx 2-Day — the perishable-shipping baseline is consistent. Andy can cite either source in the contract review and the math will hold.

Recommended next steps before the contract conversation

  • Confirm the March 2026 onboarding wave with MRG — which roll-out program, what cadence is expected next?
  • Pull the 16 contracting locations by name; ask MRG whether shelf space was reduced or assortment was deliberately edited.
  • Build the SKU-expansion ask: which 3–5 additional Vosges items pilot well in airport travel-retail? The 7-SKU set is the constraint.
  • Investigate the 30-order in-flight pipeline (Apr+May FB orders not yet shipped); confirm whether this is normal cadence or an emerging fulfillment delay worth flagging.
  • Lock in the data definition: ship-to identity = (Name, City, State, Zip, Country) tuple. Repeatable on demand from Fishbowl + audited against ShipStation Supabase.
