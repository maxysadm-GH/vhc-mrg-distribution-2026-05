07 · Read-Out

What the data is telling us.

01

Distribution depth is genuinely expanding.

Sixty-nine percent of locations show a positive SKU-breadth slope — not from one outlier, but as a broad trend. Forty-eight new ship-tos came online in the most recent three months, while only eight from the opening window have gone dormant. Both axes are moving the right direction.

02

March 2026 was a step-change.

Thirty-two ship-tos activated in a single month — more than any prior month. This is a discrete onboarding wave from MRG. Worth confirming which roll-out cycle this corresponds to and whether the same cohort is on track to add SKU breadth from their initial pull.

03

The assortment is the ceiling.

Seven active SKUs explains the chart. Five 3oz bars carry 84% of volume; the 6PC caramel and 4PC truffle do the rest. Pink Himalayan Salt Caramel leads at 22%. Real depth growth requires adding SKUs to the airport assortment.

04

Concentration is healthy.

HHI of 230 is well below the 1,500 "low concentration" threshold. The top single location accounts for 6.1% of volume; top twenty stores for 58%. No single airport closure can collapse the account.

05

The numbers reconcile.

Fishbowl issued 232 orders to MRG/WH Smith ZIPs since July; ShipStation/Supabase confirms 229 shipments. The 3-order gap is the in-flight pipeline. Carrier mix is 79% FedEx 2-Day. Andy can cite either source in the contract review and the math will hold.

Recommended next steps before the contract conversation

  • Confirm the March 2026 onboarding wave with MRG — which roll-out program, what cadence is expected next?
  • Pull the 16 contracting locations by name; ask MRG whether shelf space was reduced or assortment was edited.
  • Build the SKU-expansion ask — which 3–5 additional Vosges items pilot well in airport travel-retail?
  • Investigate the 30-order Apr+May in-flight pipeline — normal cadence or emerging fulfillment delay?
  • Lock in the data definition: ship-to identity = (Name, City, State, Zip, Country) tuple. Repeatable from Fishbowl + audited against ShipStation.
