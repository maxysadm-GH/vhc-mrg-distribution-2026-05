What the numbers say
Five takeaways.
01
Underlying growth is real, not just a longer window. Comparing the identical Oct–May period in both years (8 months vs 8 months), net sales rose +32.8% and order count +9.0% — the account is buying both more often and bigger.
02
March ’26 was the standout month — $18,886 across 58 orders, roughly triple a typical month. It coincides with the large MRG onboarding wave seen in the distribution analysis.
03
Two new SKUs added $18.3K with no prior-year base — the Red Hawaiian Salt Caramel 6PC ($10,710) and Maison Truffle 4PC ($7,560). Both carry premium ASP and are pure incremental revenue.
04
The core bar lineup is all up double-digits. Pink Himalayan, Matcha, Coconut & Banana, Barcelona and Bacon each grew year over year — the catalog is deepening, not just churning.
05
Revenue stays well diversified across doors. The top location (Shorepoints–EWR) is just ~$4.9K of $86.9K; growth is broad-based across airport markets (EWR, SFO, SEA-TAC, BWI, DEN, ORD).
* Basis & data note

Net sales = order subtotal (product revenue, excluding freight & tax), sourced from Fishbowl for WH Smith / MRG (customer #74), excluding voided/canceled orders. WH Smith order history in Fishbowl begins 3 Oct 2024, so the prior fiscal year is a partial eight months. To keep the comparison honest, the headline year-over-year figure compares only the identical Oct–May window present in both years (8 mo vs 8 mo) — not a full year against a partial one. A raw full-period total would overstate growth and is not used here.
